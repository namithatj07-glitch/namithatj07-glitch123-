01 / ABOUT

I learn by building, testing and fixing things until they work.

I'm an ECE student who treats every circuit as a question and every prototype as the answer so far. From a breadboard idea to a routed PCB, I like seeing how the physical and the digital meet.

My work moves between hardware and code: sensors that read the world, microcontrollers that decide, and interfaces that make it useful for real people.
